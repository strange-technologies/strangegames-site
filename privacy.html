Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Strange Games ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with Escape the Backrooms: Run! (the "Game").

Information We Collect

The Game does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location during normal gameplay.

If you choose to contact us for support, we receive whatever information you include in that message (for example, your email address and a description of your issue). We use this only to respond to you.

Automatically Collected Information

Like most apps distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play, the Game may receive aggregated, anonymized diagnostic and usage data (such as crash reports and app performance metrics) through the platform's built-in analytics tools (e.g. App Store Connect Analytics, Google Play Console). This data is provided by the platform and is not used by us to identify you individually.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to support requests and bug reports
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the stability and performance of the Game

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your information. We do not share information with third parties except as required to operate the Game (for example, the app distribution platform itself) or as required by law.

Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes as the Game adds new features (such as accounts, in-app purchases, or advertising), we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to strange_technologies@proton.me.
